Street tiles  /tiles/manhattan-20261103.pmtiles
  Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL):
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Cut from the Protomaps basemap daily build of 2026-11-03 (schema 4.15.2), Manhattan,
  bounding box -74.03,40.69,-73.90,40.89, zooms 0 to 15, with the pmtiles tool:
    pmtiles extract https://build.protomaps.com/20261103.pmtiles manhattan.pmtiles --bbox=-74.03,40.69,-73.90,40.89
  Protomaps basemaps: https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps (BSD-3-Clause)
